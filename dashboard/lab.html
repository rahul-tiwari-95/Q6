HOW TO READ THIS

Adaptation and retention are different measurements.

Improvement in B measures adaptation. Performance on the same A test maps after learning B measures retention. Returning to A shows recovery.

The frozen agent keeps its A-trained weights. The continuing learner keeps updating. The active rule is visible to both.
